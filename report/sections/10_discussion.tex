\todo{Rewrite in own words once the GPU sweep (rotation\_length) is in. CPU preview: the rotational SSM

short-horizon shortcut instead of the group rotation. Hypothesis for the length dependence: the loss
depends on $\cos(k\theta)$ for $k\le T$, so its landscape in $\theta$ oscillates faster as $T$ grows.

(approached exponentially), whereas a rotation angle must hit an interior value exactly.}
