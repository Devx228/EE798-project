\paragraph{Failure case 3: expressible but not learned (rotational SSM on $\mathbb{Z}_3$).}
\todo{Rewrite in own words. Evidence: the rotational SSM can represent $\mathbb{Z}_3$ exactly (unit
test: $\theta=2\pi/3$ returns the state after 3 steps). Trained at length 16 / 32 / 64 it reaches
1.000 / 0.995 / 0.719 at its training length (Table~\ref{tab:rotation-length}); at 64 two of three seeds
fail. No configuration generalises: at length 512 all are at 0.38--0.43 (chance 0.33). Reading the
layer-1 angles off the weights (Fig.~\ref{fig:rotation-angles}) shows the learned rotations are not
multiples of $120^\circ$, tokens 1 and 2 receive nearly the \emph{same} angle in every pair (e.g.\
$-136.7^\circ$ vs.\ $-134.8^\circ$), so the rotation cannot even distinguish them, and part of the memory
decays (0.63--1.00). The network learned a short-horizon shortcut rather than the group rotation (GPU
checkpoint, training length 16, seed 0: 1.000 at length 16, 0.544 at 64). Hypothesis for the length dependence: the loss depends on
$\cos(k\theta)$ for $k\le T$, so its landscape in $\theta$ oscillates faster as $T$ grows.}

\paragraph{Failure case 4: $\mathbb{Z}_3$ resists every model, although $S_3\supset\mathbb{Z}_3$ does not.}
\todo{DeltaProduct$_2$ ($\beta\in(0,2)$) generalises on $S_3$ (0.91 at 512, all seeds) yet not on
$\mathbb{Z}_3$ (0.51), even though $\mathbb{Z}_3$ is the rotation subgroup of $S_3$. Tentative
explanation, to be stated as a hypothesis: in $S_3$ the transpositions can be implemented by single exact
reflections ($\beta\to2$, a saturating parametrisation), and the 3-cycles then arise as their products;
in $\mathbb{Z}_3$ every non-identity token must itself be a rotation by exactly $\pm120^\circ$, which needs
an exact interior angle between two keys. Same pattern as negative eigenvalues vs.\ rotation angles.}

\paragraph{Methodology lesson: a training budget that is too short ranks models wrongly.}
\todo{Rewrite in own words. First recall sweep (results/recall\_capacity\_v1: length 512, vocabulary 4096,
4k steps): even attention stayed at 12--30\% with 8 pairs and accuracy was non-monotonic in the number
of pairs (SSM $N{=}64$: 41\% at 8 pairs, 99\% at 16). Recall is learned in a sudden jump, so a fixed small
budget measures \emph{whether a run jumped in time}. Calibration (16 pairs, length 256, up to 20k steps,
early stopping) gives every model $\ge$99\%, with steps-to-90\%: attention + short conv 500, Gated
DeltaNet 1500, SSM 5500, plain attention 6500. A short causal convolution, which supplies the previous
token for free, makes attention learn the lookup $\sim13\times$ faster; we therefore use it as the
attention baseline for the capacity sweep.}
